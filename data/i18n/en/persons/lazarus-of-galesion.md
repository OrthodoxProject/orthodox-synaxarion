Venerable Lazarus was born around 968 in Asia Minor. After years of pilgrimage and of monastic life in the Holy Land, he returned to the region of Ephesus and struggled as a stylite on Mount Galesion.

Disciples gathered around his pillar, and several monasteries were built on the mountain. He reposed on 7 November 1053, and his life was written by his disciple Gregory.
